Jack Calculator

Arithmetic, GT, Tax±, MU, rounding and thousands separators — an Android calculator that needs no sign-up.

What the app does

  • Arithmetic: decimal-based, no floating-point drift.
  • Commercial keys: GT total, Tax+/Tax-, MU markup.
  • Rounding & display: F/CUT/UP/5-4 rounding, fixed decimal places, thousands-separator toggle.
  • Three languages: Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English; can follow the system.
  • No account, no sign-up, no in-app purchases. Free, supported by ads.
